Sleep Noise

Sounds for sleep, focus and calm. Mix them, set a timer, switch off the screen.

Get it on Google Play

What you get

  • Noise coloursWhite, pink, brown, grey, blue and violet noise, generated live on your device.
  • NatureRain, ocean waves, wind, a gentle stream, a waterfall and birdsong.
  • CozyCrackling fireplace, winter blizzard, a purring cat and two kinds of clocks.
  • Mix your ownPlay several sounds at once and set each volume separately. Your last mix is remembered.
  • Sleep timer15, 30, 60, 90 minutes or your own. The sound fades out gently.
  • Built for the nightDark theme, a black night screen, playback with the screen off, pauses when headphones are unplugged.

Simple and private

No account, works offline, free and supported by unobtrusive ads. The app does not collect personal data. Read the privacy policy.

Savings Challenge

Turn saving into a game: 100 envelopes, 52 weeks, 365 days or your own goal. Fill the grid, colour the picture, keep the streak.

Coming soon on Google Play

What you get

  • Popular challenges100 envelopes, 52 weeks (also reversed), 365 days (fixed or growing) and a custom goal split into random fields.
  • Colouring progressA pixel-art picture is coloured in as you save. Piggy bank, house, car, beach, gift and Christmas tree.
  • StreaksKeep your streak alive. Missed one period? Watch a short ad to save it.
  • RemindersOne reminder, daily or weekly, at the time you choose. Asked only when you want it.
  • Any currencyAmounts in your local currency, 21 languages.
  • Just tickingNo expenses to enter. Tick a field and see your progress.

Simple and private

No account, works offline, free and supported by unobtrusive ads. Your challenges stay on your device. The app does not handle money and gives no financial advice. Read the privacy policy.
