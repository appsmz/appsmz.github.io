Sleep Noise

Sounds for sleep, focus and calm. Mix them, set a timer, switch off the screen.

Get it on Google Play

What you get

  • Noise coloursWhite, pink, brown, grey, blue and violet noise, generated live on your device.
  • NatureRain, ocean waves, wind, a gentle stream, a waterfall and birdsong.
  • CozyCrackling fireplace, winter blizzard, a purring cat and two kinds of clocks.
  • Mix your ownPlay several sounds at once and set each volume separately. Your last mix is remembered.
  • Sleep timer15, 30, 60, 90 minutes or your own. The sound fades out gently.
  • Built for the nightDark theme, a black night screen, playback with the screen off, pauses when headphones are unplugged.

Simple and private

No account, works offline, free and supported by unobtrusive ads. The app does not collect personal data. Read the privacy policy.
